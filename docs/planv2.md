# Light Handler v2: the ideas.md redesign, built by the agent team

## Context

The game is complete and passing (title, 3 levels, boss, ending). `docs/ideas.md` replaces most of its design: a colour-wheel weapon with five rays, eleven enemy types in five classes, one city square shared by every level that loses detail era by era (the Handler's condition worsening), a dash, continuous spawning, a new boss, and "16-bit" art.

Your answers: **24 to 36 hours left**, **double resolution** (640x360, 32px tiles), continuous eras end on a **survive timer**, keep **Field Guide, pause page map, hidden secrets, hearts and mercy**, and add a **Settings page** with a **demo mode** (all levels unlocked), with secrets **more hidden** (only the cracked wall shows).

`docs/rules.md` stays the hard constraint:

| Rule | How this plan keeps it |
| --- | --- |
| 10-15 minute loop | Era timers are fixed, so length does not depend on skill: about 12.5 min (table below). |
| All 3 themes: Comic, Twist, Light | Comic eras, the arrest twist kept, light as the weapon. |
| Design and idea are yours | `docs/ideas.md` is the source. Anything I had to invent is listed under "Decisions for you" and goes in `docs/DESIGN.md` for you to edit. |
| Fresh code, no kits; assets free and credited | Still all hand-written; art and audio still generated by our own code. No new third-party assets. |
| AI disclosed; history audited | `CREDITS.md` stays accurate. You commit before we start and after each wave. I never commit. |

## The design, as I read ideas.md

**Structure (about 12.5 minutes):**

| Era | Look | Spawning | New this era | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover + intro | - | - | Red, Blue, Green rays | 0.5 |
| 1 Golden | Bright sun, every detail | 4 waves | Swarm and armour enemies | 2.5 |
| 2 Cyberpunk | Dusk, neon, fewer details | Continuous, 2:15 timer | Speed enemies; Blue taken away; dash (3s) | 2.25 |
| 3 Retro | Dark, dull, fewer still | Continuous and dense, 2:15 | Stealth enemies; overdrive; wheel locked and rotating; double dash (5s); UV lens | 2.25 |
| 4 Manga | Black and white, outlines only | Continuous bullet hell, 2:15 | Projectile enemies; Unprism upgrade; locked to White | 2.25 |
| 5 Boss | Swaps era per attack | The Prism | - | 2 |
| Ending | Full detail returns, plain daylight | - | The arrest twist | 1 |

**Weapon:** the EMW Machine has three modes on one key (RGB, UV, Unprism) and a colour wheel at the bottom right rotated with a key.

| Ray | Shape | Multipliers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | Cone in front, hits everything in it | 1x all |
| Red | Straight laser, strongest up close, falls off with distance | 1.5x armour |
| Green | Charged blob that slows what it touches | 2x speed |
| White (Unprism) | 360 degree pushback of enemies and projectiles | 1.5x swarm and projectile, 0.25x others |
| UV | Cone that reveals stealth enemies and halves their health | 0x others |

**Enemies by class:** swarm (rat, slime, bat that flies over obstacles), armour (Ironclad, rolling Golem that blocks rays for others), speed (zig-zag bat, skittish one that dashes away when hit), stealth (ghost, aggressive ghost), projectile (snowman leaving slowing ice, acid slime leaving poison), and the Prism boss (two dashes, then a third attack that switches the era and its weapon rule, announced over its head beforehand).

**Detail loss:** the same square and the same buildings in every era, drawn with less each time, so the player sees the world draining. In the ending the full detail comes back.

## Decisions for you (my defaults, edit in DESIGN.md)

- **Controls:** WASD move, mouse aim, left click fire, Q/E rotate the wheel, F switch mode, Space dash, Esc pause.
- **Dying in a timed era** restarts from the last 45-second checkpoint, not the whole era, so the length rule holds.
- **Secrets:** one per era, in a building wall of the square; only a hairline crack shows and one specific ray breaks it.
- **Real identities** for the six new enemy types in the twist (the old ones keep theirs). I will propose them; they are your call.
- **Settings:** music volume, sound volume, screen shake, demo mode (era select on the cover). Saved in the browser.
- **Naming:** radiation types become "rays"; the Noir style is replaced by Cyberpunk and Retro.

## What is reused and what is replaced

- **Reused:** scene flow (`Boot`, `Title`, `Game`, `Ending`, `UI`), the event bus (`src/events.ts`), `Progress` (`src/state.ts`), the UI kit in `src/ui/` (HUD, captions, cards, hit words, pause page, Field Guide), the audio synth in `src/audio/`, pathfinding (`src/systems/Navigation.ts`), wave spawning (`src/systems/WaveDirector.ts`), the art baker (`src/art/index.ts`), hearts and mercy, and the whole test setup (`tests/`, `tools/smoke.mjs`).
- **Replaced:** the four radiation types (`src/systems/EMWMachine.ts`, `src/config/radiation.ts`), the monster roster (`src/entities/`, `src/config/monsters.ts`), all level data (`src/config/levels/`), all sprite data (`src/art/sprites/`), and the world scale (`src/config/world.ts`: 640x360 at zoom 2, every distance and speed doubled).

## How it gets built

Same method as before: I set contracts and a running placeholder build first, then agents work in parallel in folders nobody else touches, and I integrate and test after each wave.

**Step 0 (you):** commit the current working game. It is the fallback if the redesign overruns.

**Step 1: Director foundation.** Rewrite `docs/DESIGN.md` from ideas.md. New contracts in `src/types.ts` and `src/events.ts` (ray ids, enemy classes, era definitions with spawn tables and weapon rules, dash, timer, settings). Rescale the world. Placeholder art and stub rays/enemies so the game boots and tests pass before any agent starts.

**Step 2: Wave A, six agents in parallel.**

| Agent | Owns | Delivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art: environment | `src/art/city/` | The city square at 32px in five versions (Golden, Cyberpunk, Retro, Manga, plain), detail dropping each era |
| Art: characters | `src/art/sprites/` | Player at 32x48 with walk and dash, 11 enemies, the Prism, their real identities, ray effects, icons |
| Audio | `src/audio/` | A Cyberpunk track, Retro reworked from the Noir track, sounds for dash, each ray, wheel, mode switch, ice, acid, timer surge |
| Weapons and player | `src/systems/EMWMachine.ts`, `src/entities/Player.ts`, `src/config/rays.ts` | Wheel, three modes, five rays, dash and double dash, era weapon rules (ray removed, overdrive, locked wheel, white only) |
| Enemies | `src/entities/`, `src/config/monsters.ts` | The 11 enemies with class multipliers, flying, rolling cover, ice and acid floors, and the Prism |
| QA | `tests/`, `tools/` | Tests and smoke test moved to the new contracts; a production-build boot check |

**Step 3: Wave B, three agents in parallel.**

| Agent | Owns | Delivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eras and narrative | `src/config/levels/`, `src/config/text.ts`, `docs/LEVELS.md` | Golden's four waves, spawn tables and timers for the three continuous eras, secret placement, intro lines, Field Guide entries, ending text |
| Flow | `src/scenes/Game.ts`, `src/scenes/Ending.ts`, `src/systems/` | Continuous spawner from street entries, timers and checkpoints, upgrades between eras, hidden secrets, boss era swaps, ending, demo-mode era jumps |
| Interface | `src/scenes/UI.ts`, `src/scenes/Title.ts`, `src/ui/` | Colour wheel at bottom right, mode and dash indicators, era timer, Settings page, era select, pause map for five eras, Field Guide for the new roster |

**Step 4: Director integration.** Full check, smoke test, my own look at every era, fixes. Then **you play it** and tune numbers.

**Step 5: ship.** Timed playthrough, checklist in `docs/TESTING.md`, build, upload, test from itch.io.

**If we fall behind, cut in this order:** second variant of each enemy class (aggressive ghost, zig-zag bat, acid slime), hidden secrets, pause page map, the boss's era-swap attack (keep the dashes), then fall back to 16px art for enemies only.

## Risks

- **Art volume is the big one.** Double resolution is about four times the pixel work, across 12 enemies and five versions of the square. It gets two agents and the first cut.
- **Balance will be untested.** Eleven enemies, five rays and three era rules will only have been played by bots when you get it.
- **Dense eras and frame rate.** Enemy and projectile counts will be capped.
- **Usage.** Nine agents is a large spend, comparable to the first build.

## Verification

- `npm run check` (typecheck, unit tests, build) after every agent hands back.
- `npm run smoke` plays every era with real input and fails on any console error; QA extends it to cover dash, each ray, each era's weapon rule, the timer and the ending.
- I look at every era, the wheel, Settings and the ending in a real browser before handing over.
- A timed human first playthrough must land in 10 to 15 minutes.
- Final pass against `docs/rules.md` line by line: three themes present, license, `CREDITS.md`, AI disclosure, itch.io build.
